Most of what I know, I taught myself. That is freeing, but it has a catch: nobody marks my work. I can finish a lab, feel clever and still not understand why it worked.

## The test

My test is simple. After a lab, I try to explain it in writing to someone who has never seen it. Where my explanation gets vague, my understanding is thin.

It happens almost every time. I think I understand a technique, then I try to write the second paragraph and realise I have been copying commands without knowing what they do.

## What changed

- I slow down on the parts I would normally skim.
- I keep notes during a session, not after, because memory edits the story.
- I publish, even when it feels unfinished. A public page is a good kind of pressure.

## Who it is for

Mainly, my future self, six months from now, staring at the same problem. Secondarily, the beginner who types the same question into a search box that I once did.

If you are early in your journey, start a notes file today. Make it ugly. Make it yours.
